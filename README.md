We don't just teach technology.

We train IT professionals.

Most IT training focuses on memorizing commands, watching videos, and passing exams.

Real IT jobs require something different.

They require people who can investigate issues, troubleshoot systems under pressure, think critically, and make decisions when things break.

401Ops was built to close that gap.

Our Mission
To help aspiring IT professionals build real-world experience through hands-on practice, guided projects, and realistic scenarios.

We believe the best way to learn IT is to do IT.

Instead of memorizing concepts, learners solve problems, respond to incidents, and build infrastructure in a safe, guided environment.

Why We Built This
Too many talented people find themselves stuck in the same cycle:

Earn certifications
Pass exams
Apply for jobs
Get told they need experience
Employers want practical skills, but many training platforms focus primarily on theory.

We built 401Ops to help bridge that gap by providing the experience learners need before they land their first IT role.

Learning By Doing
Every feature on the platform is built around a single principle:

Learn by doing.
Instead of reading about DNS, you'll troubleshoot a DNS outage.

Instead of memorizing Active Directory commands, you'll onboard employees and manage enterprise environments.

Instead of watching another Linux tutorial, you'll connect to a server and resolve real operational issues.

Every learner is paired with an AI mentor designed to emulate the guidance of an experienced senior engineer.

The mentor doesn't simply provide answers.

It:

Encourages critical thinking
Guides troubleshooting processes
Explains concepts in context
Reinforces best practices
Adapts to individual learning styles
Think of it as having an experienced engineer looking over your shoulder while you learn.
