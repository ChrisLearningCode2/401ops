We don't just teach technology.
We train IT professionals.
Most IT training focuses on memorizing commands and passing exams. Real IT jobs require people to understand systems, investigate problems, troubleshoot under pressure, and make decisions. We built this platform to close that gap.

Our Mission
To help aspiring IT professionals build real-world skills through hands-on practice — not just memorization. We believe the best way to learn IT is to do IT: solve real problems, work through real incidents, and build real infrastructure in a safe, guided environment.

Why We Built This
We've seen too many talented people stuck in a cycle: they earn certifications, pass exams, and still feel unprepared for real IT work. Employers want experience. Entry-level jobs ask for skills you can only get on the job. We built a way to get that experience before you land the role.

Learning by Doing
Every feature on this platform is designed around one principle: learning by doing. Instead of reading about DNS, you troubleshoot a DNS outage. Instead of memorizing AD commands, you onboard 50 new employees. Instead of watching a video about Linux, you SSH into a server and fix a disk-full alert.

Real-World IT Skills
We cover the skills that matter in enterprise environments: Active Directory, DNS, DHCP, Linux administration, network troubleshooting, file servers, incident response, and more. Every concept is taught in the context of how it's used on the job — not just how it works in theory.

The AI Mentor
Every learner gets a personal mentor — an experienced senior engineer who guides them through concepts, projects, and incidents. The mentor doesn't just give answers; it asks questions, reinforces critical thinking, and adapts to each learner's strengths and growth areas. It's like having a senior engineer looking over your shoulder.

From Help Desk to Administrator
We built this platform for the people who keep the world's computers running: Help Desk technicians, Tier 1 and Tier 2 support analysts, career changers, and aspiring administrators. Whether you're aiming for Systems Administrator, Network Administrator, Linux Administrator, or DevOps Engineer, our learning paths guide you from where you are to where you want to be.

Our Vision
We envision a world where anyone with curiosity and determination can build a career in IT — without a four-year degree, without expensive bootcamps, and without already knowing someone in the industry. We're building the apprenticeship program we wish we had when we started.

Privacy by Design
We teach privacy. We should practice it.

401 Academy believes learning shouldn't require surrendering unnecessary personal information. We collect only the information we reasonably need to operate the platform, provide your learning experience, protect your account, process payments, and meet applicable obligations. We don't collect information simply because we can. If we don't need it, we'd rather not have it.
